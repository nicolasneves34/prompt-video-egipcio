Estrutura de Cenas
Cena 01
Abertura épica — Vista aérea do deserto
0:00 – 0:15

Câmera começa muito alta, acima de nuvens douradas ao pôr do sol. Mergulha em velocidade épica em direção ao deserto. Areia voa. Revela um salão decorado com tema egípcio — corredores com hieróglifos nas paredes, tochas acesas, obeliscos na entrada.

Cena 02
Entrada pelo corredor — Tour de primeiro pessoa
0:15 – 0:35

Câmera entra pelo corredor largo decorado com pinturas egípcias nas paredes, padrões de ouro e lápis-lazúli, tochas crepitando. O chão tem areia. Ao fundo, uma luz dourada pulsa — a pirâmide se revela lentamente no final do corredor.

Cena 03
Reveal da Pirâmide — Momento épico
0:35 – 0:50

Câmera sai do corredor e a cena abre dramaticamente. No centro, uma grande pirâmide construída de caixas douradas. Em volta, um canal circular de água azul — o Rio Nilo. Quatro estandes nos cantos em forma de X. Câmera gira lentamente em órbita ao redor da pirâmide.

Cena 04
Tour pelos estandes — Close dramático
0:50 – 1:20

Câmera voa de estande em estande com cortes cinematográficos:
· Tumba — sarcófago dourado com vasos canópicos, tochas, mistério
· Pirâmide — mini pirâmide, papiros com hieróglifos, olho de Hórus
· Mesa — comidas típicas, pão, vasos de barro, uvas, ambientação
· Faraó — estátua majestosa sentada no trono, cajado e mangual

Cena 05
Plano aéreo completo — Visão da rotatória
1:20 – 1:40

Câmera sobe lentamente, revelando toda a rotatória vista de cima. O Nilo brilha ao redor da pirâmide. As pessoas caminham em sentido anti-horário pelos estandes. Luz dourada inunda a cena. Partículas de areia flutuam no ar.

Cena 06
Encerramento épico — Logo do evento
1:40 – 2:00

Câmera recua lentamente enquanto faíscas douradas explodem da ponta da pirâmide. O sol se põe atrás da estrutura. Fade com título do evento aparecendo em hieróglifos que se transformam em texto moderno. Música épica cresce até silêncio.

Prompt Completo para o Gemini

📋 Copie e cole no Gemini Video

Generate a 2-minute cinematic video with epic, trailer-style editing mixed with an immersive first-person tour. The setting is an Egyptian-themed event hall built by students. VISUAL STYLE: Cinematic 4K, warm golden lighting, dramatic shadows, sand particles floating in air, torch flames flickering. Color palette: deep golds, sandy beiges, lapis lazuli blues, deep blacks. Camera movements are smooth and epic — sweeping crane shots, slow orbit rotations, and immersive first-person walking. SCENE 1 — EPIC OPENING [0:00–0:15]: Start with an extreme aerial view above golden sunset clouds. Camera dives at high speed toward a decorated hall in the middle of a desert setting. Sand flies dramatically. Reveal the entrance: two tall obelisks flanking a wide corridor decorated with Egyptian paintings and hieroglyphs on the walls. Torches burn on both sides. Epic orchestral music begins. SCENE 2 — CORRIDOR TOUR [0:15–0:35]: First-person camera walks slowly through a wide decorated corridor. Walls covered in colorful Egyptian murals — pharaohs, gods, hieroglyphic inscriptions in gold and blue. Sand on the floor. Torches crackle. At the end of the corridor, a warm golden light pulses. The silhouette of a massive pyramid slowly comes into view. SCENE 3 — PYRAMID REVEAL [0:35–0:50]: Camera exits the corridor. The scene opens dramatically wide. Center: a large pyramid built from stacked golden boxes, glowing under warm light. Surrounding it: a circular canal of shimmering blue water — the Nile River. Four exhibition stands arranged in an X pattern around the pyramid. Camera slowly orbits the pyramid in a majestic 180-degree arc. Music reaches a powerful crescendo. SCENE 4 — STAND TOUR [0:50–1:20]: Fast cinematic cuts between the four stands, each with a dramatic close-up: — STAND 1 (Tomb): A golden sarcophagus with canopic jars, torch light, mysterious shadows, Egyptian death mask detail. — STAND 2 (Pyramid Info): Mini golden pyramid, papyrus scrolls with hieroglyphs, Eye of Horus symbol glowing. — STAND 3 (Food Table): Ancient Egyptian foods — flatbread, clay pots, grapes, figs, ceramic bowls arranged beautifully. — STAND 4 (Pharaoh): Majestic pharaoh statue seated on throne, holding crook and flail, golden crown, cobra uraeus detail. SCENE 5 — AERIAL OVERVIEW [1:20–1:40]: Camera rises slowly from ground level to full aerial view. The entire rotunda is revealed — circular Nile canal glowing blue around the central pyramid, four stands in X formation, people walking counterclockwise. Golden dust particles float in rays of light. The scene feels ancient and majestic. SCENE 6 — EPIC CLOSING [1:40–2:00]: Camera slowly pulls back while golden sparks burst from the pyramid's apex like fireworks. The sun sets dramatically behind the pyramid, creating a perfect silhouette. Title text appears: first as hieroglyphs that transform into modern letters. Music builds to a powerful finish, then fades to silence. Final frame: the pyramid glowing gold against a deep orange sky. AUDIO: Epic orchestral soundtrack — strings, drums, and horn instruments. Dramatic buildup through scenes 1–3, intense energy in scene 4, majestic and wide in scene 5, emotional and grand finale in scene 6. QUALITY: Cinematic 4K, 24fps, film grain texture, anamorphic lens flares, depth of field, volumetric lighting, photorealistic rendering.
